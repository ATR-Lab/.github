  <b>The Advanced Telerobotics Research Lab at Kent State University is an innovation research laboratory focused on exploring the frontiers of telepresence robotics, tele-embodiment, autonomy and artificial intelligence.</b>

We build robots that people operate from a distance and Physical AI (AI that senses and acts in the real world through a robot) that works alongside them. Students from high school through doctoral study do the work on real hardware: humanoid, quadruped and social robots, robot arms and immersive teleoperation interfaces.

## What we work on

| Area | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Extending human presence** | Robots people operate and inhabit at a distance: telepresence, tele-embodiment and immersive VR and AR teleoperation |
| **Physical AI that works with people** | Service robots, social robots and humanoids that sense, decide and act in real settings alongside people |
| **Learning by building** | Students at every level do research on real hardware and test it in competition |
| **The next generation of innovators** | Hands-on robotics and Physical AI programs for middle and high school students and their teachers |

## Current projects

| Project | What it is | Code |
|---|---|---|
| **Expressive Motion** | Learning expressive humanoid locomotion from monocular runway videos, on a Booster K1 humanoid | [expressive-motion](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/expressive-motion) |
| **Coffee Buddy** | An interactive service robot for coffee shops with voice interaction, facial expressions and head tracking (ROS 2) | [kami-head](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/kami-head) |
| **PepperKit** | A SoftBank Pepper robot stack that answers through a realtime large language model voice agent | [PepperKit](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/PepperKit) |
| **VendoBot** | A projection-augmented delivery robot for intent transparency and motion legibility, published in the HRI 2026 Companion proceedings | [Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3776734.3794461) |
| **Unitree Go2 workspace** | A ROS 2 workspace for the lab's Unitree Go2 robot dog: vision, human interaction and video streaming | [go2_ws](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/go2_ws) |

## Tools we share

- **[ATR Lab design skill](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/atr-lab-design-skill)**: the lab's brand system (logos, colors, fonts, templates and checking scripts) packaged as a Claude skill.
- **[ROSEnv](https://github.com/MarcoDotIO/rosenv)**: a ROS 2 project and dependency manager with persistent Docker environments and lockfiles, built by a lab member.

## Work with us

- **Kent State students:** read [Getting started](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/getting-started-atr-lab), then visit the [lab website](https://www.atr.cs.kent.edu/) to find out how to join.
- **Prospective graduate students:** see the lab website and apply through Kent State University graduate admissions.
- **Contributors:** every repository follows the [contributing guide](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [code of conduct](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) unless it has its own.
