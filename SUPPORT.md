# Getting help

> **Read this when** you are stuck, have a question about an ATR-Lab repository or want to reach the lab.

| You are | Your question is about | Ask here |
|---|---|---|
| A lab member | Code, robots, lab equipment | [Slack channel], or the repository's issues |
| Anyone | A bug or a missing feature in a repository | That repository's **Issues** tab |
| Anyone | ROS 2 in general | [All things ROS 2](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/all-things-ros2), then [Robotics Stack Exchange](https://robotics.stackexchange.com/) |
| A prospective student, sponsor or partner | Joining or working with the lab | The [lab website](https://www.atr.cs.kent.edu/) |
| A journalist | Interviews and media requests | Kent State University media relations |

When you ask for help with code, include what you ran, what you expected and the full error message. For robot
problems, include the robot, its firmware or software version and the ROS 2 distribution.
