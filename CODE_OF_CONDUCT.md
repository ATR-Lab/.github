# Code of conduct

> **Read this when** you take part in any ATR-Lab repository: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions or
> comments. A repository with its own `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` overrides this page.

The Advanced Telerobotics Research Lab at Kent State University builds robots that help people rather than
replace them, and we want our online spaces to work the same way: people first, work second.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Be welcoming to newcomers. Explain terms, link to docs and remember that everyone started somewhere.
- Give credit. Name the people whose work, data or ideas you build on.
- Be honest about results. Don't overstate what a robot, a model or a study can do.
- Respect privacy. Don't post other people's personal information, photos or study data.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or imagery, in public or in private messages.
- Sexualized language or content, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or events.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every ATR-Lab repository and in any space where someone represents the lab online.
Lab members are also bound by the
[lab's code of conduct](https://github.com/ATR-Lab/getting-started-atr-lab/blob/master/lab-rules/code-of-conduct.md)
and by Kent State University policies, including the
[Student Code of Conduct](https://www.kent.edu/studentconduct/code-student-conduct).

## Reporting

Report a problem to [conduct contact email]. Reports are read by [conduct contact role] and handled as
confidentially as the situation allows. If the concern involves that person, contact the lab director,
Jong-Hoon Kim, associate professor of computer science, at <jkim72@kent.edu>.

For discrimination, harassment or safety concerns, Kent State University's own reporting routes also apply;
the lab will point you to the right university office.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may restrict or block
anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously. Decisions are explained to the person involved when it is safe to
do so.
